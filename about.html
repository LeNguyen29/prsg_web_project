ABOUT

Welcome To PRSG

PRSG is a fan-made MMORPG game on Roblox that aims to deliver the most authentic premium Power Rangers experience that it can. The game takes inspiration from various RPG games and features an open world map with story driven quests.

Developed by Fragmented Studios

Development began in 2021, driven by a group of passionate fans who have long dream of an RPG style Power Rangers game. Since then, the project has went through numerous challenges, along with multiple iterations and reimaginings, until a clear creative vision is reached that defines the game today.

KEY FEATURES

Morphing & Roll Call

Morph into your favorite suit with show-inspired transformation sequence and effects. With each featuring their respective roll call poses. Choose from a wide selection of rangers that spans across multiple eras.

KEY FEATURES

Combat & Abilities

Every Ranger comes with their own signature weapons and abilities, inspired by the shows and comics. With a wide variety of playstyles and powers to choose from, there is something for every type of player.

KEY FEATURES

Open World Map

Travel freely across a variety of regions with different biome, from sprawling city to desert town, and iconic Tokusatsu landmarks. Explore and uncover hidden secrets, complete quests, and have fun with your friends.
